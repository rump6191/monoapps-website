Overview

SleepGuard is a sleep analysis app that records and analyzes your sleep through the night using only your smartphone's microphone and accelerometer. Without a smartwatch, sleep band, or any other wearable device, simply place your phone by your bedside and it records your snoring, breathing sounds, and tossing and turning, then screens your risk of obstructive sleep apnea (OSA). It was built to help you catch warning signs early and decide whether a visit to a sleep clinic may be worthwhile.

Using it is simple. Start sleep tracking before you go to bed and place your phone by your bedside, and the app analyzes sounds and movement throughout the night. When you wake up and end the session, you get a report that shows your sleep score, sleep efficiency, estimated number of apnea events, sleep stages, and more at a glance. Risk is presented in easy-to-understand levels: normal, low, caution, and high.

As your daily records accumulate, you can see how your sleep health changes through daily and weekly trends. SleepGuard is a wellness and screening app, not a medical device, so it provides risk assessment and a recommendation to consult a professional rather than a diagnosis. It is currently in preparation for launch and will be available soon on iOS and Android.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to your questions about SleepGuard

How does SleepGuard measure sleep?

Start tracking before you fall asleep and place your phone by your bedside. The microphone records your snoring and breathing sounds, and the accelerometer records your tossing, turning, and movement for analysis. No separate wearable device is required.

Can it diagnose sleep apnea?

No. SleepGuard is a wellness app that screens for risk, not a medical device, and it does not provide a diagnosis. If your risk is rated high, we recommend an accurate examination at a medical facility, such as polysomnography (PSG), along with a professional consultation.

Do I need a wearable device?

No, you don't. Measurement is done with just your smartphone, without a smartwatch or sleep band. All you need to do is place your phone by your bedside.

How are the recorded sounds handled?

Sleep analysis is designed to be processed on the device. Detailed information about how data is processed and stored will be provided in the privacy policy released together with the launch.

When will it launch?

SleepGuard is currently in preparation for launch. You can be the first to hear the launch news on the MonoApps News page.
