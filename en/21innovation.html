Overview

21Innovation is a smart product comparison web platform that gathers product information sold on Coupang into one place so you can compare it easily. More than 1,757 products are organized systematically by category and subcategory (product group), letting you quickly find items of the same type and compare them side by side. It is free to use right in your web browser, with no installation required.

It is ideal for anyone who wants to carefully weigh the specs, pros, and cons of several products before making a sensible purchase. Even if you are buying an unfamiliar item such as an appliance, a piece of furniture, or a digital device and aren't sure what to look for, the organized comparison tables and per-category buying guides make it easy to decide.

Each product includes price, brand, and model along with detailed specifications such as energy efficiency grade, capacity, size, weight, color, country of origin, warranty period, special features, and release year. You can place up to 4 products from a single product group side by side and see the price range at a glance, with each product's key advantages, disadvantages, and purchase reviews summarized for you. Products you like link straight to their Coupang purchase page.
