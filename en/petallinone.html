Overview

PetCare is an all-in-one health management app for dog and cat owners. You can log daily meals, water, bowel movements, activity, and condition, track weight changes on a graph, and record GPS-based walks with route, distance, and time all in one place.

Register multiple pets and manage each one's information separately, and keep up with vaccination records and upcoming appointments through reminders. The app also includes everyday care tools such as a feed calculator, an age converter, breed information, and a location-based vet hospital search.

The AI Health Analysis lets you photograph stool or skin and returns a status (normal, warning, or danger), findings, advice for the owner, and whether a vet visit is recommended. This analysis is for reference only and is not a medical device — please consult a veterinarian for an accurate diagnosis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about PetCare

What is PetCare?

PetCare is an all-in-one app for managing your pet's walks, health, and vaccinations. It offers GPS walk tracking, a feed calculator, weight tracking, vaccination reminders, AI health analysis, vet hospital search, and breed information.

Can I use it for both dogs and cats?

Yes. You can manage both dogs and cats, and the app provides traits and care notes for 80 dog breeds and 54 cat breeds.

Which devices can I use it on?

PetCare is available on both iOS and Android, with downloads on the App Store and Google Play.

Is it free to use?

Yes, PetCare is free to download and use. For more frequent AI health analysis and an ad-free experience, you can subscribe to PetCare Pro.

How does AI health analysis work?

A single photo lets the app analyze your pet's skin and stool conditions so you can catch early warning signs. With a Pro subscription you get up to 50 analyses per month.
