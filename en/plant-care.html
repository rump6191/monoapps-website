Recognize Plants with Photos

Select a photo from your camera or gallery and AI will automatically identify the plant species. Check the plant name and scientific name along with the recognition rate.

  • Automatic scientific and common name display
  • Recognition rate percentage
  • Various icon selection available

Weather-Based Watering

Automatically adjusts watering schedules by reflecting your current location's temperature and humidity in real time. Check at a glance with the D-day countdown.

  • Real-time temperature, humidity & weather integration
  • Watering D-day countdown

  • Disease name & confidence score display

  • Treatment & prevention guidance
